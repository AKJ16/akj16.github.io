01 — ABOUT

About Me

Learn More About Me ↗

Hello! I'm Adriel Kevin Jonathan, a Computer Science student passionate about game design and game development, with a particular interest in creating engaging gameplay experiences and turning ideas into playable projects.

I enjoy exploring how gameplay, mechanics, and storytelling can come together to create memorable player experiences. Through competitions and personal projects, I've gained experience in team leadership, game design, prototyping, and development.

I'm always looking for new ideas to explore, new skills to develop, and new projects to build. My goal is to create games that are not only enjoyable to play, but also leave players with an experience worth remembering.

01
GAME 2026

EcoRangers

EcoRangers is a fast-paced 2D arcade, educational puzzle game where players must keep up with an endless stream of waste and sort each item correctly. The game was developed by a five-person team over four months.

I contribute as the Game Designer and Project Manager, developing the game’s concepts, core gameplay, and mechanics while overseeing the development process to ensure the project is completed on time and delivers a satisfying player experience. I also contribute as a Game Programmer, handling the technical aspects of development and implementing gameplay systems and mechanics.

GAME JAM 2026

GridMancer

GridMancer is a 2D tactical puzzle game developed as an entry for the Compfest 2026 Game Jam, themed “Order in Disorder.” Created by a three-person team in 3 days, the game challenges players to strategically place unique weapons on a limited grid to defeat incoming enemies.

I served as the Game Designer and Project Manager, leading the initial concept development, core gameplay loop, and game mechanics. I also managed the project’s scheduling and task distribution while contributing to the technical development of the game as Game Programmer.

GAME 2026

MindScope

MindScope is a psychological deduction simulation game that immerses players in the role of a psychologist, challenging them to diagnose fictional psychological conditions by analyzing clues and applying critical and analytical thinking.

The prototype was developed by a four-person team over a two-month period. I contributed primarily as the Game Designer and Project Manager, overseeing the game’s conceptualization, core gameplay loop, and gameplay mechanics. I also managed the project schedule, coordinated task distribution among team members, and monitored the team’s progress to ensure milestones were met within the development timeframe. In addition, I contributed as a 2D Artist and Audio Designer, creating and implementing visual and audio assets to support the game’s atmosphere and overall player experience.
