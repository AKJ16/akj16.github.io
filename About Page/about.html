01 — ABOUT ME

About Me

I'm Adriel Kevin Jonathan, a Computer Science student and Game Designer passionate about creating meaningful and engaging gameplay experiences.

My interest in game development comes from the combination of creativity and technology. I enjoy taking an idea from its earliest concept and turning it into something players can actually experience.

As a Game Designer, I focus on gameplay, mechanics, player experience, and the overall direction of a project. I’m particularly interested in how individual mechanics interact with one another and how they can be shaped into engaging and satisfying gameplay loops.

Throughout my projects, I’ve also taken on responsibilities beyond game design, working across programming, project management, 2D art, and audio. This broader experience has given me a better understanding of the development process as a whole and helps me communicate effectively with teammates across different disciplines.

I especially enjoy experimenting with game mechanics and exploring combinations that might not seem like they naturally belong together. I’m fascinated by the challenge of mixing different genres, systems, and ideas to see whether they can come together in an interesting way. Even when an idea seems difficult or unconventional, I enjoy prototyping it, discovering what works and what doesn’t, and finding ways to make the pieces fit into a cohesive experience.

My goal is to continue growing as a Game Designer while creating games that leave a lasting impression on the people who play them.
