GAME PROJECT

Every patient has a story.

Step into the role of a Wayfinder and uncover the stories hidden behind every patient. MindScope is a psychological deduction simulation where players examine clues, connect information, and carefully assess fictional conditions in a world where every case demands a different way of thinking.

03 — DESIGN APPROACH

Why MindScope?

Mental health remains a complex and often misunderstood field, with symptoms sometimes dismissed as weakness or attention-seeking. This lack of awareness can lead to stigma and misconceptions.

MindScope aims to address this by providing an interactive and accessible way to explore mental health conditions. Inspired by the gameplay and decision-making mechanics of Papers, Please and That’s Not My Neighbor, the game presents a simplified and fictionalized diagnostic experience where players analyze symptoms, review information, and make decisions about fictional characters’ mental conditions.

While MindScope is not a substitute for professional psychiatric practice or a tool for real-world diagnosis, it is a fun and educational experience designed to promote basic understanding and awareness of mental health.
